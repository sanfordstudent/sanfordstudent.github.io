# Welcome 

I am an Assistant Professor of Educational Statistics and Research Methods at the University of Delaware. I conduct methodological research in educational measurement and psychometric/quantitative methods, and contribute to applied research in diverse areas including educational assessment, early literacy, child language development, cognitive decline in older adults, student motivation, and more. I teach classes in educational and psychological measurement, item response theory, and structural equation modeling. The [My Work]({{ "/mywork/" | relative_url }}) page contains links to research products.
